1

Create your account & log in

Open the app in any modern browser on your computer, tablet or phone.

New here?

  1. Click Create an account.
  2. Enter your first name, last name, work email and a password.
  3. Submit, and you're taken straight to your dashboard.

Already have an account?

  1. Enter your email and password on the Welcome back screen.
  2. Click Log in.
  3. Click your initials at the top right for Settings, Subscription and Log out.
2

Set up your brand

A brand is a business you post for. Running more than one? Add each one as its own brand and switch between them from the menu at the top of every page.

  1. Go to Brands in the left menu and click Add brand.
  2. Brand identity: name, description, logo, primary and secondary colours.
  3. Contact: website, email, phone, address and timezone. Scheduled posts go out in this timezone.
  4. Content defaults & AI voice: default tone, language, hashtags, call to action, keywords, words to avoid and emoji usage.
  5. Team: invite colleagues by email to work on this brand with you.
Tip: The more you fill in under Brand voice, the more your AI-written posts will sound like you. For example: "Friendly experts who explain things simply. Optimistic, never pushy."
3

Connect your social accounts

Connect each account once and post to all of them from one screen.

Your password stays with you. You sign in on the official Instagram, Facebook, Google or LinkedIn page. We never see or store your password, and you can remove access at any time.

How to connect

  1. Pick the right brand from the menu at the top.
  2. Open Social Accounts in the left menu.
  3. Click Connect on the platform you want.
  4. Sign in on the platform's own page and click Allow.
  5. You'll come back here and the card will show Connected. Use Add another to link more pages or channels.

What each platform needs

IG

Instagram

A Business or Creator account linked to a Facebook Page. Personal accounts can't be connected.

f

Facebook

You must be an admin of the Facebook Page. Posts appear as the Page, not your personal profile.

▶

YouTube

A Google account with a YouTube channel. Videos are uploaded to that channel.

in

LinkedIn

Posts go to your personal profile. Ask us if you'd like to post to a company page.

Account shows "Expired"? This happens if you change your password or after about 60 days on LinkedIn. Click Reconnect and sign in again. Nothing else is lost.
4

Create posts & reels

Use Create Post for images and carousels, or Create Reel for short vertical videos.

  1. Choose platforms: tick the connected accounts you want to post to.
  2. Write your caption, or click AI Generate to get a draft with hashtags and a call to action.
  3. Add media: use Upload / Library, or create visuals with AI image and AI video.
  4. Fine-tune per platform under Platform-specific content, e.g. a shorter caption for one network.
  5. Check the Preview to see how the post will look on each platform.

Then choose what to do

Save draft

Keep working on it later.

Schedule

Pick a date and time and it goes out automatically.

Publish now

Send it live straight away.

Tip: For reels you can add a hook, voice-over and subtitles. Tick Also share reel to feed so it shows on your Instagram grid too.

AI Studio & planning tools

Everything else you need to keep your content running smoothly.

✨ AI Studio

Enter a topic (e.g. "Diwali offer on billing software"), your audience, offer, tone and language. Get ready-to-use captions, images or reel scripts for each platform.

📅 Calendar

See every scheduled and published post by day, week or month. Click a post to schedule or reschedule it.

🎯 Campaigns

Group related posts, like a festive sale or a product launch, under one campaign with an objective, and track them together.

🖼️ Media Library

Drag and drop your images and videos, sort them into folders and find them by name or tag when you build a post.

📊 Analytics

Track reach, engagement and followers. Compare platforms and see your best-performing content. Click Sync now for the latest numbers.

💳 Subscription

See your current plan, how much of each limit you've used this month, and upgrade at any time.

Common questions

Quick fixes for the things people ask about most.

The Connect button is greyed outThat platform hasn't been switched on for your workspace yet. Contact support and we'll enable it.
Google says "Access blocked"Your Google account needs to be approved first. Send us the email you use for YouTube.
My Instagram account won't connectSwitch it to a Business or Creator account in the Instagram app, link it to your Facebook Page, then try again.
A post failed to publishOpen it from Published or All posts, read the message shown, fix it (often an expired account or a file that's too large) and click Retry failed.
My scheduled post went out at the wrong timeCheck the timezone in your brand settings. Posts are scheduled in the brand's timezone.
How do I stop posting to an account?On Social Accounts, click the bin icon next to it to disconnect. You can also remove access from your Facebook, Google or LinkedIn settings.
